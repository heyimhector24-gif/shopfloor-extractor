THE PROBLEM

01

Paper forms don't scale

Work orders and inspection sheets are still filled out by hand on the floor, then re-typed later — twice the work, twice the chance for error.

02

Generic OCR guesses

Most extraction tools output a confident-looking number even when the source was smudged, crossed out, or ambiguous. A wrong quantity or spec looks identical to a right one.

03

Bad data hides until it's a problem

A misread field usually isn't caught until it causes a shipping error or a failed audit — long after anyone could have just looked at the original page.

OUR APPROACH

If it can't read a field with confidence, it flags it — instead of guessing.

Every extracted field is either something the model read clearly, or something it's telling you to check yourself. There's no in-between where a guess is dressed up as a fact.

PART NUMBERPN-2205-B
QUANTITY38 VERIFY MANUALLY
Flagged — handwriting unclear, could be 3 or 8.

HOW IT WORKS

STEP 1

Photograph or upload the document

Snap it on your phone right on the floor, or upload a scan later.

STEP 2

Fields get matched to a template

Work order, QC checklist, or incoming inspection — each has its own field set.

STEP 3

Confident fields fill in, unclear ones get flagged

Anything below a clear read gets a visible flag and a one-line reason why.

STEP 4

You confirm or correct, then export

Tap a flagged field to fix it. Export clean data to CSV when you're done.
